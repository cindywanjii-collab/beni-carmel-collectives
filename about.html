OUR STORY

BLESSED
WITH
PURPOSE.

Béni Carmel Collectives was created from a desire to build something that represents more than what we wear.

The name Béni comes from the French word for blessed. Carmel carries the symbolism of Mount Carmel — a place associated with abundance, growth and significance.

Together, Béni Carmel represents a belief that we are blessed with something meaningful to carry, create and share with the world.

Every piece is therefore more than an item of clothing. It is an expression of identity.

WHAT BÉNI CARMEL MEANS

FAITH WOVEN
INTO EVERY THREAD.

We believe what you wear can communicate who you are, what you believe and where you are going. Béni Carmel exists to create pieces that carry meaning while remaining authentic, contemporary and wearable.
